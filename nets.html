Platonic Nets

Each solid unfolded flat. Solid lines are cuts, dashed lines are folds, and the small trapezoids are glue tabs - one for every pair of edges that meets again when it closes. Print the page, or save a single net as SVG to scale it to whatever size you like. Each unfolding was chosen by searching every spanning tree of the faces for one where no two faces land on top of each other.

Platonic Solids is open source on GitHub with an MIT license.
